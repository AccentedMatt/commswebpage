The story

From a local start to proper business connectivity.

BallyComms started in Ballynahinch after seeing how often small businesses struggle with slow internet, patchy WiFi and networks that have grown without a plan.

The focus is simple: keep your business connected and covered whether you are a farm, a café shop, a workshop or a small office. That means checking the site, designing a sensible WiFi or CCTV layout and keeping the firewall maintained.
