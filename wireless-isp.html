Why fixed wireless?

A workable option for the right site.

Fixed wireless internet uses installed equipment to link your premises to network infrastructure. For the right site, it can be a good alternative when standard services are too slow, unavailable or unreliable.

BallyComms keeps the process straightforward: check the location, confirm suitability, install the equipment and support it after it goes live.
