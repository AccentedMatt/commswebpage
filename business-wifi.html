How it works

A practical WiFi design process.

The first step is understanding the premises. A farm office, a café shop and a workshop all use WiFi differently. Card machines, CCTV, guest phones, staff devices and tills should not all be treated the same.

After that BallyComms can recommend a layout, supply the right network equipment, set up the guest and staff networks, then plan firewall maintenance so the setup is easier to keep on top of.
