For businesses where connection problems cause real disruption.

Common problems

Signs it might be time to review your setup.

“The WiFi only works in one part of the building.”

Coverage gaps often come from poor placement, unsuitable equipment or a network that has grown without a plan.

“Our broadband options are limited.”

Fixed wireless may be worth checking when standard services do not match the needs of the premises.

“The cameras are hard to access or keep dropping.”

CCTV works better when the recorder, cameras, switches and remote access are planned as part of the network.

“The router and firewall have never been reviewed.”

A scheduled update plan can keep the edge of the network in better shape without turning it into a full helpdesk contract.
